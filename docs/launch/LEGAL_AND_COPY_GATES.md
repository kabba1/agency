# Legal And Copy Gates

This project needs legal review before a real token launch. This file is a product checklist, not legal advice.

## Required Review

- Token name, ticker, icon, description, and site copy.
- Risk disclaimer, terms, privacy notice, and moderation policy.
- Wallet login copy and purchase-to-agent wording.
- Any reference to Pump.fun features, agent mechanics, or marketplace roadmap.

## Copy Rules

- Describe Agency as a meme/social simulation and entertainment product.
- Say a qualifying purchase can create a simulated resident when that feature is actually live.
- Avoid claims that the token is an investment, produces profit, pays yield, shares revenue, creates dividends, or guarantees future utility.
- Do not describe agents as financial products or owners of legal/economic rights.
- Avoid comparing the product publicly to Minecraft or using Minecraft-like branding.

## External References To Recheck Before Launch

- Pump.fun fees: https://pump.fun/docs/fees
- Pump.fun terms: https://pump.fun/docs/terms-and-conditions
- Pump.fun tokenized-agent disclaimer: https://pump.fun/docs/tokenized-agent-disclaimer
- SEC Staff Statement on Meme Coins: https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/staff-statement-meme-coins

These pages can change. Recheck them in the final launch week before publishing public copy.

## Hard No-Go Conditions

- Legal review is incomplete.
- Public copy implies expected profit or guaranteed financial outcome.
- Admin token is bundled into production web code.
- Real chain ingestion cannot replay missed events.
- Wallet-private details are visible to anonymous users.
- Snapshot export and restore do not pass.
